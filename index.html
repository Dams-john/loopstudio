Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trading on CryptoVault

How do I get started?
Simply create an account, verify your email, and you can start depositing and trading cryptocurrencies immediately. The sign-up process takes less than 2 minutes.
What cryptocurrencies can I trade?
CryptoVault supports Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, and 100+ other cryptocurrencies across multiple blockchain networks including ERC-20, TRC-20, and BEP-20.
How secure is my investment?
We use bank-grade encryption, cold storage for 98% of assets, mandatory two-factor authentication, and maintain an insurance fund to protect your investments.
Are there any hidden fees?
No hidden fees. We offer transparent, competitive pricing with no deposit fees and low trading fees. All costs are clearly displayed before you confirm any transaction.
How long do withdrawals take?
Withdrawal requests are typically processed within 24 hours. Once approved, the funds are sent to your wallet and confirmed on the blockchain network.
Is CryptoVault regulated?
Yes, CryptoVault operates in full compliance with international financial regulations and undergoes regular third-party security audits to ensure transparency and safety.
